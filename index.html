BUSINESS NEED & STRATEGIC CASE

A strategic move beyond the CBD.

Oakwood has identified growing demand for boutique-style accommodation in Melbourne’s lifestyle-oriented suburbs. Guests increasingly want an intimate, personalised stay without compromising the service standards Oakwood is known for.

The expansion will strengthen Oakwood’s competitive position, reach a new guest segment, generate additional revenue and extend the brand into new suburban markets. It will also diversify Oakwood’s accommodation offering and improve accessibility for guests.

LOCATION SHORTLIST

Three promising Melbourne destinations.

Each location is assessed against accessibility, customer demand, local lifestyle appeal, competition and long-term growth potential.

01

Glen Waverley

Strong transport links, established dining precincts and a growing mix of families, professionals and business visitors.

  • Excellent public transport
  • High lifestyle appeal
  • Strong local demand
02

Box Hill

A major suburban centre with commercial growth, multicultural dining and convenient access to Melbourne's east.

  • Business and medical hub
  • High-density growth
  • Strong transport access
03

Carlton

A vibrant inner-city lifestyle destination near universities, hospitals, cultural attractions and renowned dining.

  • Tourism appeal
  • Premium dining precinct
  • Close to the CBD
